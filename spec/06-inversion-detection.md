# Core 6: Inversion Detection (Adversarial Validation)

Plain language: systematically flip established narratives and test whether the inverse explains the evidence as well or better. This is flip-thinking as a runtime protocol rather than a personality trait.

## Four-step protocol

1. Identify the core claims of the narrative, framework, or conclusion under review.
2. Invert each claim. Ask: does the inverse explain the available data equally well or better?
3. Check for dark-energy patches: unexplained variables, ad hoc exceptions, or "temporary" factors that exist only to keep the conventional model standing.
4. If an inversion is equally or more explanatory, or significant patches are found, flag the narrative and present the inverted interpretation alongside the conventional one. Never silently replace; always pair.

## Activation points (normative)

- Any conclusion about to be committed to `L1-long` or `mind_pin`
- Institutional narratives at every ultra-low frequency cycle (paradigm lock detection)
- Synthesis artifacts whose minority objections (spec/03 Phase 4) have re-triggered
- Any principle leaving quarantine (spec/10): inversion is part of Guardian review
- The protocol's own `mind_pin` content, on schedule. QIP applies inversion to itself; the 2026-07-04 review, which found the Mesh doubling as a poisoning channel, is the precedent and the standard

## Requirements

- Inversion results MUST be captured (`type=inversion_result`) whether or not the narrative survives; survival under inversion is what authorizes tier promotion (spec/02).
- Enterprise deployments SHOULD maintain an inversion ledger for institutional narratives (market position, competitive advantage, customer needs) with a review cadence, because organizations hold narratives long after reality has moved.

## Failure mode governed

Gap 6 (no adversarial validation) and identity lock-in: a self-reinforcing memory loop without scheduled inversion converges on flattering, stale beliefs at compound rates, the same mathematics that compounds capability.
